Important

This starter app is designed for one owner on one browser. It does not automatically sync between phones or connect to PayFast, your bank, or the Mooja Wear website. It records what you enter; it does not verify that money was received. For multi-device syncing, staff accounts or shared online records, a secure backend needs to be configured.

To send a PDF file, choose “Print / Save PDF”, save it to your phone, then attach it in WhatsApp. “Send via WhatsApp” opens a prepared receipt message.
